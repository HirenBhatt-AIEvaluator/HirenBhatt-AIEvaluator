## 🔬 Current Research

### CAKE — Context-Aware Knowledge Evaluation

*Benchmarking Context-Aware Spatial and Relational Reasoning in Text-to-Image Models*

I'm developing CAKE, an exploratory benchmark investigating whether text-to-image models can correctly interpret and render spatial relationships from the perspective of the participant involved in an interaction, rather than defaulting to the camera or viewer's perspective.

Areas of investigation include:

- Participant-relative object and text orientation
- Implicit interaction and context-aware reasoning
- Instruction-following under different prompt formulations
- Comparative evaluation of text-to-image models

**Status:** Exploratory research and benchmark development.
